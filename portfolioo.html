I'm a frontend developer who enjoys turning ideas into interfaces people actually want to use. I started out tinkering with HTML and CSS out of curiosity, and it turned into a habit of picking things apart to see how they're built.

These days I spend most of my time with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I'm always adding new tools to that list. I like projects that force me to think about performance and accessibility, not just how a page looks on my own screen.
